# Research question

MVP 0 studies the following question:

> Under scarcity, do delegation-based decision mechanisms improve survival and welfare compared to direct voting?

The project compares four simple mechanisms:

1. majority voting
2. approval voting
3. expert delegation
4. simplified liquid democracy

The model is deliberately minimal. Its purpose is to provide a reproducible baseline that can later be extended with richer coalitions, strategic manipulation, deliberation, and formal guarantees.
